Welcome to Ben's Mobile Mechanic Truck!

The future of automotive vehicle service is here!

The analogy of the web being today’s Main Street, comes from the concept where a lot of businesses are located on a city’s Main Street. They all have storefronts to advertise their products, so that people can come and buy their services and interact with the store workers. The web serves as a similar function where people can go to purchase products and services while interacting with the website or with someone else. I think that this analogy goes good with my business idea of doing a mobile mechanic truck because the web would be one of the most important ways to advertise. Since there is no store front and it eliminates customers having to bring their vehicles to a shop and instead, bringing it to them, the best way for customers to interact with this service would be through the internet’s global reach. For my domain name system, I made it short and easy to remember and showing that it is a small and locally owned business. By utilizing the website to expand my business and I can increase trust and professionalism by posting reviews.
