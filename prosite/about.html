About Us - Our Orgin Story

Learn about how Mountain Bean Cafe got started

Mountain Bean Café first started in 2016 with one goal in mind, how do we create a coffee shop that is convenient for both locals and tourists? We came up with a plan to offer a membership subscription, giving locals and visitors the opportunity to enjoy the benefits of being a member of our café. We offer an exclusive membership lounge where you can connect, relax, and unwind. Whether you have been working all day or exploring the mountains, our coffee shop offers a place to start or end your day. Conveniently located in the mountains of Colorado, we are accessible to the many hiking trails and outdoor adventures the area has to offer.

This is exactly why we started Mountain Bean Café, so that everyone, no matter where they come from, can feel at home. By offering convenient subscription memberships and some of the finest roasted coffee Colorado has to offer, we hope to create an experience worth coming back to. Come enjoy the taste of the Rocky Mountains with coffee prepared by our dedicated and professional baristas. Our café offers more than just coffee—it offers a true Colorado mountain experience.
